by Sean Opao

If there are occupational hazards over my abilities, it's ADHD.
Naturally there are a lot of inconveniences that will cost me over
time - that even when it's fun drawing as you learn a new thing, it
still can be difficult to comprehend its every geometry of it.
Because the human body is complex. memorizing for the first time
involves following a guide as you draw by multitasking; keeping it
in mind of them having layers as you stack them together and once
you render the interior of the body with the skin; you situate
where you intend to brighten and darken up that part. It's indeed
a lot of work but I can then finally express different poses with
ease. I can confidently say I can recall visually the other parts
of the body but it's often distinctive compared to reality and by
its sizes, that even when I try to embrace my failures it all
still prevents me to keeping on going as it's hard to
instinctively recognize the parts which I'ven't yet nailed it in
my head. Being unhappy about it can always hinder how expressive I
wanted to be so I have barely regained my mojo until the other day
I came to the conclusion that the bones are vital to know, which I
could've done sooner in order to succeed making it right. Not to
mention the tendons, or where to start expanding the veins. Don't
want to repeatedly fix them errors forever. Figuring them out
would be even more confusing - as much as doing it with some
logical shortcuts like Loomis method, constructing the proportions
with measurements, which are all anathema to me that made me feel
getting worse.
