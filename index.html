by Sean Opao

as for my credentials, i'm barely self-disciplined. i only draw when i'm
inspired, but not inspired most of the time so there has only been one
of my works you'll see here that're completed. i'm primarily a
graphite artist and mostly used pens, but i've been motivated to use
traditional colors which the period of renaissance/baroque masters had
access to and wanna do more detailed paintings; the face in general
is pretty smooth so the shadows and light will "evaporate like
vapor" - one of the reasons why Leonardo is famous.
