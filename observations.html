Observations Journal

by Sean Opao

If there are occupational hazards over my abilities, it's ADHD.
Naturally there are a lot of inconveniences that will cost me over
time - that even when it's fun drawing as you learn a new thing, it
still can be difficult to comprehend its every geometry of it.
Because the human body is complex. memorizing involves following
your subconscious as you draw by multitasking; keeping it in mind
of them having layers as you stack them together and once you
render the interior of the body with the skin; you situate where
you intend to brighten and darken up that part. It's indeed a lot
of work but once it's become an ease for me, I can finally express
different poses. I can confidently say I can recall visually the
other parts of the body but it's often distinctive compared to
reality. I have barely regained my mojo until the other day I came
to the conclusion that the bones are vital to know, which I
could've done sooner in order to succeed creating the right
anatomy. Not to mention the tendons, or where to start expanding
the veins. Figuring them out would be even more confusing - as much
as doing it with some logical shortcuts like Loomis method,
constructing the proportions with measurements, which are all
anathema to me that made me felt getting worse.

There've been attires I have always picked up where it
came from the Prussian Era and some any other timelines while the
main theme of a work is western frontiers. I would of course doubt
about it automatically as I'm still not very much an adept in
fashion and creating original characters. However, I keep that
metacognition with me to breathe in and doubt later on, then keep
trying and failing not unlike with all the grammatical errors
I've written down in these pages. Sure my dyslexia could have been
playing a role.
