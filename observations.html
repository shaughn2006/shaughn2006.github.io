Observations

by Sean Opao

Drawing the hands, especially when gripped is difficult, and
figuring it out would even be more difficult. Hands have been fun
for me for a while but there's a lot that's new to me which I'ven't
yet nailed it from my head. Other angles of head, and all that. So
whenever I encounter that, I mindlessly avoid looking at it while
sketching over to prevent making a mess, and instead I have to face
somewhere else to continue being able to be fully hypnotised over
the image of the hand I'm holding from my memory.
